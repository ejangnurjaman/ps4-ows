Version 2.0.0 update of PS4 OWS:
- Added Select Method feature (Default Toolkit vs Custom).
- Default Toolkit supports multiple verified ZIPs (ps4ows.zip, ps4ows.rawgame4.v1.zip, ps4ows.raw13g.v1.zip) with SHA-256 verification.
- Custom method supports hosting directly from internal storage folder (/sdcard/ps4ows).
- Improved UI, documentation, and stability.
